# Restaurant Operations Guide

Use this guide for `PEACHES_FOOD` branches:
- `Peaches`
- `Cafe-Li`

This guide is for normal daily operation, not setup or admin work.

## 1. Before Starting the Shift

- Log in using your assigned account.
- Confirm the branch badge at the top matches your branch.
- Confirm the shift status panel shows the correct shift and date.
- Confirm you are in the correct branch before entering any stock or sales.

## 2. Receive Raw Items

Go to `Store Stocks` -> `Receive Drinks` / stock receiving area.

- Enter any raw items received for the shift.
- Confirm quantity and total cost before saving.
- Save each received item once only.

Use this when:
- new stock has been delivered
- stock is being topped up during the day

## 3. Kitchen Operations

Go to `Kitchen Ops`.

- Select the finished products produced in the kitchen.
- Enter the quantity prepared.
- Save the kitchen output.

Important:
- only post actual kitchen production
- do not guess or double-enter output

## 4. Daily Sales

Go to `Daily Sales`.

For each selling item:
- review `Opening`
- review `Added`
- enter the `Bal Qty` / closing quantity left at the end of the shift

The system uses stock movement to calculate sold quantity.

Important:
- enter physical closing balance, not guessed sold quantity
- check items carefully before proceeding to reconciliation

## 5. Finance Reconciliation

Go to `Financial Reconciliation`.

Enter:
- cash counted
- M-Pesa figures
- expenses
- debts given
- debts paid
- any other required totals

Before closing:
- confirm totals make sense
- check variance if shown
- do not close until figures are reviewed

## 6. Shift Close

Close the shift only after:
- stock balances are entered
- kitchen output is complete
- finance reconciliation is complete

Important:
- each shift must close cleanly before the next one continues
- closing stock becomes next shift opening stock
- closing cash carried forward becomes next shift opening cash

## 7. Transfers

Use `Store Stocks` -> `Transfers` only when sending stock to another branch.

- choose the correct destination branch
- enter the correct quantity
- save once

Do not use transfers for normal receiving or daily selling.

## 8. Staff Rules

- Work only in your assigned branch unless you are an authorized manager.
- Supervisors, cashiers, and chefs should not switch branches.
- Report mistakes immediately instead of creating workarounds.
- Do not enter duplicate receipts, duplicate kitchen output, or duplicate finance entries.

## 9. End-of-Shift Check

Before leaving the shift, confirm:
- all received stock is entered
- all kitchen production is entered
- all closing balances are entered
- finance is reconciled
- shift is closed successfully

## 10. When Something Looks Wrong

- stop before closing the shift
- check branch, shift, and quantities again
- inform the manager or system admin immediately

Do not force totals to match by guessing values.
