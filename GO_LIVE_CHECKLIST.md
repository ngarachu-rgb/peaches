# Go-Live Checklist

Use this on launch day for a controlled rollout.

## 1. Access Check

- Confirm each user can log in.
- Confirm each user lands in the correct restaurant and branch.
- Confirm supervisors cannot switch branches.
- Confirm managers can switch only within their restaurant.
- Confirm Bar users do not see Food data.
- Confirm Food users do not see Bar data.

## 2. Branch Setup Check

- Confirm branch badge shows the correct branch on each page.
- Confirm shift status panel shows the correct branch and shift.
- Confirm branch shift mode is correct:
  - `Peaches` = `2` shifts
  - `Cafe-Li` = `1` shift
  - `Peaches Bar` = `1` shift
  - `Cafe-Li Bar` = `1` shift

## 3. Stock Reset and Opening Balances

- Reset live stock balances to zero before opening day.
- Re-enter actual stock quantities in the app.
- Confirm prices are unchanged after reset.
- Confirm raw/store stock is correct per branch.
- Confirm Bar full-bottle stock is correct.
- Confirm Bar measured items are ready for issue-to-shots.

## 4. Food Branch Dry Run

- Receive one stock item.
- Post one kitchen output item.
- Enter one sales closing balance.
- Record one expense if needed.
- Close shift.
- Confirm next shift opens correctly.

## 5. Bar Branch Dry Run

- Receive one full bottle/can item.
- Issue one bottle to shots.
- Confirm issue history records correctly.
- Enter one direct full-unit sale balance.
- Enter one measured-item balance.
- Close shift.
- Confirm stock deductions and carry-forward behave correctly.

## 6. Finance Check

- Confirm total sales matches counted activity.
- Confirm M-Pesa opening, closing, and withdrawals reconcile.
- Confirm cash at hand is entered correctly.
- Confirm expenses and debts save correctly.
- Confirm variance is understandable before final close.

## 7. Reports Check

- Preview one shift report.
- Preview one financial report.
- Preview transfer history.
- Preview raw items received.
- Confirm branch scope works correctly for managers.

## 8. Launch-Day Discipline

- Start with trusted users only.
- Keep paper backup for the first live day.
- Report errors immediately instead of working around them.
- Do not change business rules mid-shift unless necessary.

## 9. High-Risk Items To Watch

- Shift close
- Carry-forward
- Branch isolation
- Bar issue-to-shots
- Bar measured stock closing balances
- Permissions

## 10. End-of-Day Review

- Review any user-reported issues.
- Compare stock results against physical counts.
- Compare finance totals against actual cash/M-Pesa.
- Fix small issues before widening rollout to more staff.
