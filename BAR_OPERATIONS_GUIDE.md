# Bar Operations Guide

Use this guide for `PEACHES_BAR` branches:
- `Peaches Bar`
- `Cafe-Li Bar`

This guide is for normal daily bar operation.

## 1. Before Starting the Shift

- Log in using your assigned account.
- Confirm the branch badge at the top matches your bar branch.
- Confirm the shift status panel shows the correct shift and date.
- Confirm you are in the correct branch before entering stock or sales.

Important:
- Bar does not use `Kitchen Ops`
- all bar movement starts from store stock

## 2. Receive Drinks

Go to `Store Stocks` -> `Receive Drinks`.

- Enter all new cans, bottles, wines, mixers, and spirits received.
- Confirm quantity and total cost before saving.
- Save each receipt once only.

Use this for:
- procurement
- stock top-up during the shift

## 3. Issue To Shots

Go to `Store Stocks` -> `Issue To Shots`.

Use this only when moving a full bottle into measured sales use.

Examples:
- issue a full bottle for `30ML`
- issue a bottle for `150ML` wine serving

Important:
- issuing to shots is not a sale
- it reduces full bottle stock
- it adds measured stock for that shift

## 4. Daily Sales

Go to `Daily Sales`.

For each item, enter the physical `Bal Qty` at the end of the shift.

Bar logic:
- full bottles and cans are sold from store stock
- `30ML` and `150ML` items are treated as measured items
- measured items depend on issued stock and balance quantity

Important:
- do not guess sold quantity
- enter the physical remaining balance
- check both full items and measured items carefully

## 5. Finance Reconciliation

Go to `Financial Reconciliation`.

Enter:
- cash counted
- M-Pesa totals
- expenses
- debts given
- debts paid
- other required values

Before closing:
- confirm totals make sense
- review any variance shown

## 6. Shift Close

Close the shift only after:
- all received drinks are entered
- all issue-to-shots entries are done
- all balance quantities are entered
- finance reconciliation is complete

Important:
- closing balance becomes next shift opening balance
- measured stock carry-forward must be accurate
- do not close until physical bar count is checked

## 7. Direct Sales vs Measured Sales

### Direct sales

These are normally sold directly from stock:
- cans
- bottled beer/cider
- full spirit bottles
- soda and water bottles
- items `250ML` and above

### Measured sales

These are measured items:
- `30ML`
- `150ML`

These require:
- stock issue from full bottle
- correct end-of-shift balance entry

## 8. Staff Rules

- Work only in your assigned bar branch unless you are an authorized manager.
- Supervisors should not switch branches.
- Do not use workarounds if stock looks wrong.
- Report issues immediately.
- Do not use `Issue To Shots` to record a sale.

## 9. End-of-Shift Check

Before leaving the shift, confirm:
- all received drinks are entered
- all bottle issues are entered
- all balance quantities are entered
- finance is reconciled
- shift is closed successfully

## 10. When Something Looks Wrong

- stop before closing the shift
- recheck branch, item, and balance quantity
- confirm whether the item should be direct-sale or measured-sale
- inform the manager or system admin immediately

Do not force values to match by guessing.
